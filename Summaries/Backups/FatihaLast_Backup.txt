Summary of session 0

Therapist: "Thank you for joining me today. I understand this is difficult. Can you share what’s been weighing on you?"

Patient: "I... I don’t know where to start. Every day is the same. The children need me, the house needs me, but I’m so tired. I snap at them, then hate myself after. I pray for patience, but it’s like Allah isn’t listening."

Therapist: "That sounds overwhelming. You mentioned guilt—can you tell me more?"

Patient:"A mother shouldn’t feel this way. My friends back home post about their lives—happy children, gatherings. But here? Nothing. My husband works all day. Sometimes I stare at the wall for hours. Is this... normal?" (pauses) "My mother says weak faith causes sadness. But I pray five times a day! Why do I still feel empty?"

Therapist: "Faith can be a comfort, but exhaustion isn’t a sin. What if Allah sent you help through these feelings, not as punishment?"

Patient: "I... never thought of it that way."

Therapist: "Let’s try something. When the guilt comes, instead of ‘I’m failing,’ could you say, ‘I’m carrying six children’s needs alone’?"

Patient: "That’s true. But what do I do? I can’t just leave them."

Therapist: "Small steps. Could we brainstorm one thing this week just for you? Even 10 minutes?"

Patient: "I miss singing. Sometimes I hum old Yemeni songs when the baby naps."

Therapist: "That’s beautiful. Let’s protect those moments. Next time, notice: ‘This is my soul breathing.’"
